## 🎮 About the Game

Big Ambitions is a role-playing business simulation developed and published by Hovgaard Games. It released on August 28, 2026, and combines management, economy, life-simulation, and open-world gameplay in a stylized version of New York.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Big Ambitions**. This trainer provides optional assistance with cash, personal needs, time, inventory, vehicles, and business operations. It is designed to reduce routine management tasks while preserving the game's core business, logistics, and progression systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with business layouts, test logistics networks, accelerate progression, build large company portfolios, or reduce the routine demands of energy, hunger, happiness, transportation, and inventory management.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Big Ambitions uses third-person and isometric movement through the city. The player can walk, sprint, enter vehicles, visit businesses, and transport goods between locations. The trainer does not replace normal movement, but time-freeze and vehicle assistance can make travel and delivery planning more convenient.

### Tools & Inventory

Inventory is central to purchasing and transporting products for businesses. Goods can be collected from suppliers, carried by the player, loaded into vehicles, stored in warehouses, and delivered to operating locations. Activate inventory assistance before moving or stocking goods, and avoid changing quantities while a delivery or purchase transaction is in progress.

### World & Environment

The game presents a city-management sandbox where the player leases properties, furnishes businesses, serves customers, hires employees, manages schedules, orders supplies, uses vehicles, and expands through increasingly automated operations. Cash and time assistance are most useful when testing new locations, business types, marketing plans, and logistics networks.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no combat gameplay in Big Ambitions. Progress is based on entrepreneurship, personal needs, property management, inventory, employees, transportation, finances, and business growth rather than fighting or weapons.
